# Master Roadmap: Aug 30, 2026 → Dec 1, 2026

## The honest math first (accountability, not hype)

- Total days available: **94 days** (Aug 30 → Dec 1 inclusive) = **13 full weeks + 3 buffer days**.
- Phase 1 (Python core): Aug 30 → Oct 17 ≈ **7 weeks (49 days)**.
- Phase 2 (DSA + LeetCode): Oct 18 → Dec 1 ≈ **6.5 weeks (45 days)**.

**Reality check on 200 LeetCode problems:** waiting until Oct 16 to start LeetCode would require
**200 ÷ 47 ≈ 4.3 new problems/day**, every single day, with zero rest, while learning brand-new
DSA topics simultaneously. Not realistic at the "understand + retain" level.

**Fix:** LeetCode starts in **Week 3** of Phase 1, spreading 200 problems over **11 effective
weeks**, dropping the peak rate to ~4/day only in the hardest 3 weeks, ~2.1/day average overall.

**Honest calibration on "mastery":** by Dec 1, strong provable mastery of Arrays, Strings,
Hashing, Linked Lists, Stacks/Queues, Binary Search; solid working competence in Trees, Heaps,
Backtracking, basic DP. **Graphs and DP will be at "confident with patterns," not deep mastery**
— that typically takes 3–6 months beyond this program. Natural Phase 3 (Dec onward) if desired.
